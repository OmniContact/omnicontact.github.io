OmniContact Chaining Meta-Skills via Contact Flow
for Generalizable Humanoid Loco-Manipulation

OmniContact uses contact flow as a compact planning-control interface. CF-Track learns a unified library of loco-manipulation meta-skills, while CF-Gen synthesizes, chains, and replans contact-flow segments for long-horizon humanoid tasks.

Overview #

Long-horizon humanoid loco-manipulation requires more than isolated skills: the robot must establish contact, move objects, transition between primitives, and recover when interaction fails.

Contact Flow

A compact representation combining sparse future body targets and time-series binary contact signals. It preserves interaction intent while remaining structured enough for online synthesis.

CF-Track

A unified low-level policy trained with contact-flow targets to execute carrying, pushing, kicking, sliding, relocation, and locomotion-style behaviors under one controller.

CF-Gen

A high-level contact-flow synthesizer that adapts skill templates to object state, chains stages in closed loop, and replans when the observed object state deviates from the plan.

Meta Skill Chaining #

CF-Gen decomposes long-horizon tasks into contact-aware stages, synthesizes each segment, and refreshes future segments with the updated object state. This closed-loop design turns reusable meta-skills into robust task-level behavior.

Recovery #

OmniContact detects failures by comparing observed and predicted object states. When the deviation exceeds a threshold, CF-Gen replans from the current state, naturally eliciting re-approach and resume behaviors.
