Live Policy Demo

This interactive demo runs the OmniContact policy directly in your browser with MuJoCo WASM and ONNX Runtime Web.

The viewer loads the G1 and box scene, builds the policy observation online, and executes policy.onnx for closed-loop control.

Policy config: static/humanoid-policy-viewer/examples/checkpoints/g1/omnicontact_policy.json
